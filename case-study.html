LIBERIS Fintech B2B UX Strategy

Partner Hub Redesign

Redesigning the core partner experience to improve clarity, reduce friction and drive measurable growth across the B2B funnel.

Role Lead Product Designer
Duration 3 months
Platform Web App
Methods Research · Prototyping · Testing
Cover Image
01

Project Requirements

Partners navigating the Liberis Hub struggled with a fragmented interface that buried key actions, surfaced irrelevant information, and offered no clear pathway through the funding journey. The goal was to untangle this complexity and create a coherent, confidence-building experience.

User Challenges

  • Partners couldn't locate key documents and status updates
  • Overcrowded dashboard created cognitive overload
  • No clear indication of where they were in the funding journey
  • High support ticket volume due to unclear UI affordances

Design Goals

  • Surface the right information at the right moment
  • Create a clear visual hierarchy and wayfinding system
  • Reduce time-to-complete key tasks by 30%
  • Increase partner confidence and reduce support load
"Partners shouldn't need a manual to use a product built for them."

User Stories & Solution Sketches

Translating research into design direction began with defining user stories that captured real behaviour and goals — not assumed use cases.

Core User Stories

As a partner I want to see all my accounts and their statuses in one place so I don't have to switch between screens. High
As a finance director I want to download documents directly from the hub without navigating to a separate portal. High
As a new partner I want to understand where I am in the process so I know what's expected of me next. Med
As an operations manager I want to receive contextual notifications so I'm only alerted when my attention is genuinely needed. Med

How Might We

HMW surface the most relevant information without overwhelming the dashboard?
HMW make document management feel effortless for time-poor users?
HMW create a progress system that works for both new and experienced partners?

06

Usability Testing

Testing Plan

6 Participants

Mix of existing partners and new users recruited via internal channels

45min Per Session

Moderated remote sessions via Maze + Zoom — think-aloud protocol

5 Core Tasks

Finding account status, downloading a document, locating support, reading progress, updating profile

Results & Iterations

Navigation structure was intuitive

5 of 6 users completed the account status task without guidance. Average time: 18 seconds vs. 72 seconds in the current product.

Progress tracker reduced anxiety

All participants noted the progress bar gave them "a sense of control" — notably, 3 had previously called support to ask where they stood.

Notification preferences needed iteration

Users wanted more granular control over alert types. We added a notification centre with category toggles in the next sprint based on this feedback.

Mobile tab bar required rethinking

On smaller screens, the bottom nav felt crowded. Iterated to a priority-based 4-item tab bar with "More" for secondary items.
