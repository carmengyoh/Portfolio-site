FAQs

A Cash Advance gives you a single lump sum of funding upfront. You pay back the advance plus one simple fixed fee, together as a single total. This is taken automatically, as a small, fixed percentage of your daily daily sales. No interest and no fixed monthly amount.

Revenue based finance means your payments flex with your sales. A set percentage of each day's takings goes towards your balance, so you pay more on busy days and less on quiet ones.

Once your Flex limit is approved it's ready to use straight away. You choose when to access funds, and once you do they typically reach your account within 24 hours.

With Cash Advance you receive the full approved amount upfront in one go. If you'd prefer to access funds in stages as you need them, Flex Advance may suit you better.
