Cash Advance / More details

Cash Advance

Cash Advance gives you one upfront amount to support a known business need, such as buying stock, covering equipment costs, repairs or refurbishment.

It may suit your business if you know what you need funding for and want the full amount available upfront.

Key points

  • One large upfront amount
  • Good for planned purchases or known costs
  • One-time fixed fee
  • Payments taken as a percentage of daily sales
Flex Advance / More details

Flex Advance

Flex Advance gives you access to funds over time, so you can use them when business needs or opportunities come up.

It may suit your business if your needs are less predictable, you buy stock regularly, or you want funds available for future opportunities.

Key points

  • Access from your Flex limit
  • Available over a 12-month period
  • Good for ongoing or unexpected needs
  • Fee applies when funds are used
  • Payments taken as a percentage of daily sales
