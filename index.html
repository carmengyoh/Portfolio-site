HOW I WORK

Understand the problem.
Create, test and learn.

I start with people’s needs and product goals, explore solutions with the team, and use what we learn to shape what comes next.

Start with people and their needs

I use research, data and team conversations to understand people’s needs, product goals and the constraints we’re working with.

Make ideas tangible

I map journeys and prototype ideas, using AI to help assemble flowcharts and build interactive prototypes with working logic so I can test how the experience responds to different choices and inputs.

Let evidence change the plan

I use research, feedback and real usage data to challenge assumptions and refine the direction, including simplifying the experience or changing priorities as I learn.
